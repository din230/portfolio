About me

I’m building a future shaped by curiosity, persistence, and systems thinking.

I am Diane Ngo, a first-generation college student studying computer engineering at Lehigh University. My work spans machine learning, digital signal processing, sensor design, and robotics. I enjoy translating messy real-world data into practical solutions and learning through hands-on technical problem solving.

From my research internship at Temple University to my work with FTC robotics and Science Olympiad, I have developed a strong interest in how engineering can improve health, enable intelligent systems, and support communities. I am especially motivated by work that combines technical rigor with meaningful impact.
